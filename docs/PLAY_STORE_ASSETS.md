# Google Play asset specification

Use the existing brand system: near-black cinematic background, electric cyan accent, deep purple, restrained glow, and identity-based copy. Do not show medical, therapy, guaranteed-outcome, or financial claims.

## Required concepts

- App icon: adaptive icon using the supplied cyan star/diamond foreground and deep-purple background in `android/app/src/main/res/drawable`.
- Phone screenshots: portrait captures of onboarding, dashboard with missions/XP, future-self chat, journal/timeline, and insights/share card. Use 1080px-class captures where possible.
- Tablet screenshots: repeat the same benefit sequence at a responsive tablet width; show the additional breathing room and two-column dashboard relationships without stretching controls.
- Feature graphic: dark cinematic gradient, cyan Alter Ego glyph, headline “Your future self is watching.” Keep text large and within safe margins.

## Recommended screenshot headlines

1. “Meet the version of you you’re building” — onboarding and Alter Ego identity card.
2. “Turn identity into today’s next move” — daily missions and XP.
3. “Ask your future self for clarity” — coaching chat with the informational disclaimer visible.
4. “See the signal in your consistency” — insights and timeline.
5. “Share the progress, not the perfection” — generated share card and Android Sharesheet.

All final dimensions, localized copy, screenshots, feature graphic, content rating, and store metadata require a Play Console review before submission.
