# Google Play listing draft

## App name

Alter Ego AI

## Short description

Build your future self through daily missions, reflection, and AI-powered motivation.

## Full description

Become the person you were supposed to be.

Alter Ego AI turns identity-based goals into small actions you can actually repeat. Define the version of yourself you are becoming, receive daily missions, and build momentum through XP, streaks, reflections, and a transformation timeline.

Features:

- Create a personal Alter Ego from your current and future identity.
- Choose the goals and motivation style that fit your season.
- Complete daily missions for XP and visible progress.
- Ask your future self for a practical next move.
- Journal wins, struggles, and lessons with guided AI summaries.
- Review consistency, categories, streaks, and transformation milestones.
- Create text and image share cards when you choose to share.
- Use daily reminders, dark mode, light mode, and responsive layouts.

Premium plans may include expanded AI coaching, deeper insights, transformation artifacts, and premium themes. Subscription availability, billing periods, prices, trials, and cancellation terms are shown by Google Play at purchase time.

Alter Ego AI is general wellness, habit-building, motivation, and lifestyle software. AI coaching is informational and motivational only.
